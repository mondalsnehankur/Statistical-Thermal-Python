# Thermal & Statistical Physics (Python)

This repository contains Python programs focused on numerical and computational approaches to problems in **thermal physics and statistical mechanics**.  
The work emphasizes translating theoretical models into simulations to study physical behavior quantitatively.

---

## 📌 Topics Covered

- Thermodynamic quantities:
  - Internal energy
  - Specific heat
  - Entropy-related calculations
- Equations of state:
  - Ideal gas
  - van der Waals gas
- Distribution functions:
  - Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution
  - Bose–Einstein distribution
  - Fermi–Dirac distribution
- Radiation and decay models
- Numerical evaluation of thermodynamic integrals

---

## 🧠 Focus of the Repository

- Connecting **theory with computation**
- Exploring **temperature dependence** of physical quantities
- Understanding **statistical behavior of large systems**
- Visualizing distributions and thermodynamic trends

The codes are written with clarity and modularity in mind, allowing easy modification for further experimentation.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Libraries

- Python
- NumPy
- SciPy
- Matplotlib

---

## 🎯 Intended Audience

- Physics students (BSc / MSc)
- Learners interested in computational statistical mechanics
- Anyone exploring numerical methods in thermodynamics

---

## 📁 Repository Structure

Each script is self-contained and focuses on a specific physical model or concept.  
Plots and numerical results are generated directly for analysis and interpretation.
